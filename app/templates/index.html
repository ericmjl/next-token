How language models actually work

One trick, explained in ten interactive beats: predict the next token, over a library bigger than any lifetime of reading. By the end you'll be able to explain the trick to your own lab, and you'll know where it breaks.

The toy model behind every demo on this page is a real language model, just a very small one. It read a few paragraphs of biology text and counted which letters follow which. Nothing here is faked, which is exactly the point.
