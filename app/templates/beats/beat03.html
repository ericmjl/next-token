Beat 03

A probability list, then a dice roll

At every step, the model does one thing: it assigns a probability to every possible next token. Prediction means picking from that list, and picking, called sampling, means rolling a weighted die. That die is why the same prompt can give you a different answer tonight than it gave this morning.

Temperature is the knob that reshapes the list before the roll. Low values make the model cautious and repetitive. High values make it adventurous and strange. When you use a chat app, the app hides this knob from you. When you call a model through its API, the way software talks to a model directly instead of through a chat window, you set it yourself, and it belongs in your methods section.
